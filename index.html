How do I get my files?

Each product is an instant digital download through Buy Me a Coffee. After checkout you download the files right away. Nothing is shipped. For the Classroom License, the answer key with grading checklists is emailed after instructor verification.

The guides are printable PDFs (US Letter). The Lean Six Sigma Toolkit and the Freelance CAD Drafting Starter Guide also include a fillable Excel workbook (.xlsx). The Green Belt Study Guide comes with a separate one-page formula cheat sheet PDF.

Who handles payment?

Payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee. When you click a Buy button you go to Robin's Buy Me a Coffee shop to check out.

Is the Green Belt guide an official certification product?

No. It is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by ASQ, IASSC, or any certification body. The questions are original, not actual exam questions, and no study aid can guarantee a result.

Can I share the files?

The paid products are licensed for personal use by the purchaser, and the Toolkit templates may also be used for your organization's internal projects. No resale, redistribution or public sharing. The Studio Team License covers the Commercial Revit Template & Standards Pack and the Freelance CAD Drafting Starter Guide for a team of up to 5, and the Office Team License covers the same two products for a team of up to 10. For 11 or more seats, email robinwolfson.drafting@gmail.com for a quote. The free quiz may be shared in its original, unmodified form.
